We're well into another exciting year of Red Raider athletics. If you're not yet member, we invite you to join or renew your membership in the Raider Athletic Booster Club!

Your support directly benefits all Tosa East athletic teams by funding essential equipment, uniforms, training resources, and more. One such benefit is the amazing Senior Posters which give our kids a child-embarrassing warm-and-fuzzy and show how much we at TE appreciate the special leadership of our senior athletes!

Booster Club members enjoy great perks too, including spirit wear vouchers, athletic season passes.

Whether you're new to the Red Raider community or a returning supporter, we’d love to have you. To learn more about who we are, what we have funded, and our special event check out our website:
