-----------------------------------------------------------

Watch for our table at in-person registration on Monday, August 11 and Thursday, August 4, where we will be answering questions, helping with memberships, and selling spirit wear.


As we gear up for another exciting year of Red Raider athletics, we invite you to join or renew your membership in the Raider Athletic Booster Club!

Your support directly benefits all Tosa East athletic teams by funding essential equipment, uniforms, training resources, and more. Booster Club members enjoy great perks too, including spirit wear vouchers, athletic season passes, and admission to our Home Opener event on August 23!

Whether you're new to the Red Raider community or a returning supporter, we’d love to have you. To learn more about who we are, what we have funded, and our special event check out our website.

If you are interested in a corporate sponsorship instead, email sponsorships@raiderboosterclub.org

Join us in making a difference for our student-athletes!

What is a Tosa East Athletic Booster? The Tosa East Boosters are the family, friends and supporters of Tosa East Athletics. The Raider Athletic Booster Club is a non-profit, all-volunteer club that works with our Athletic Director to support needs that are not fully covered by the annual school budget. Each month, the board takes requests from coaches and the Athletic Director to support needs that are out of the school budget.

Why buy Booster Club spirit wear?
All proceeds from the sale of our spirit wear go directly
to the TE Raider Athletic Booster Club, which is a 501
(c)(3) non-profit organization of parents, friends and
alumni who help promote and encourage all student
athletes and athletic programs. The Booster club has
provided thousands of dollars of support for athletic
programs at Tosa East since 1981. Some examples of
what the Booster Club has recently funded are:
scholarship opportunities for our athletes, the new
scoreboards that were recently installed in the gym,
senior posters/banners for your athlete to keep at the
end of their senior seasons and all the new updates to
the concession stand. And that's just to name a few!
