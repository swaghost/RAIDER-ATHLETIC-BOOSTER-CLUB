WHAT IS A BOOSTER, WHO ARE WE?
---------------------------------------
The Tosa East Boosters are the family, friends and supporters of Tosa East Athletics. The Raider Athletic Booster Club is a non-profit, all-volunteer club that works with our Athletic Director to support needs that are not fully covered by the annual school budget. Each month, the board takes requests from coaches and the Athletic Director to support needs that are out of the budget.
