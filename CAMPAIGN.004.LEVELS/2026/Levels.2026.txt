
As we gear up for another exciting year of Red Raider athletics, we invite you to join or renew your membership in the Raider Athletic Booster Club!

Your support directly benefits all Tosa East athletic teams by funding essential equipment, uniforms, training resources, and more. Booster Club members enjoy great perks too, including spirit wear vouchers, athletic season passes, and admission to our Home Opener event on August 22nd!

Whether you're new to the Red Raider community or a returning supporter, we’d love to have you. To learn more about who we are, what we have funded, and our special event check out our website:  
www.raidersboosterclub.org

Join us in making a difference for our student-athletes!
